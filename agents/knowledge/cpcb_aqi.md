# Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) - National Air Quality Index (NAQI)

Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/air-quality-index/
Retrieval Date: 2026-11-15

## Overview
The National Air Quality Index (AQI) was launched by the Central Pollution Control Board (MoEFCC) to disseminate air quality status in an easily understandable format (one number, one colour, one description). The index considers eight parameters: PM10, PM2.5, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, and Pb.

## AQI Categories and Breakpoints
The sub-index for a given pollutant concentration is calculated using linear interpolation across designated breakpoint ranges:

| AQI Category | AQI Range | PM2.5 (24-hr avg, µg/m³) | PM10 (24-hr avg, µg/m³) | NO2 (24-hr avg, µg/m³) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Good | 0 - 50 | 0 - 30 | 0 - 50 | 0 - 40 |
| Satisfactory | 51 - 100 | 31 - 60 | 51 - 100 | 41 - 80 |
| Moderate | 101 - 200 | 61 - 90 | 101 - 250 | 81 - 180 |
| Poor | 201 - 300 | 91 - 120 | 251 - 350 | 181 - 280 |
| Very Poor | 301 - 400 | 121 - 250 | 351 - 430 | 281 - 400 |
| Severe | 401 - 500 | 250+ | 430+ | 400+ |

## Associated Health Impacts
- **Good (0-50):** Minimal impact. Air quality is considered satisfactory, and air pollution poses little or no risk.
- **Satisfactory (51-100):** Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people including children, the elderly, and those with pre-existing lung or heart disease.
- **Moderate (101-200):** Breathing discomfort to the people with lungs, asthma, and heart diseases. May cause slight throat irritation in healthy individuals.
- **Poor (201-300):** Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure. Significant exacerbation of symptoms in heart and lung patients.
- **Very Poor (301-400):** Respiratory illness to the people on prolonged exposure. Pronounced effect on people with lung and heart diseases. Outdoor physical exertion should be avoided.
- **Severe (401-500):** Affects healthy people and seriously impacts those with existing diseases. Emergency advisory: avoid all outdoor activity; vulnerable groups should stay strictly indoors with clean air measures.
