# Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) - Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) for Delhi-NCR

Source URL: https://caqm.nic.in/grap-schedule
Retrieval Date: 2026-11-15

## Structure of GRAP
GRAP is an emergency response mechanism triggered dynamically based on prevailing and forecast air quality in Delhi-NCR. It is categorized into four progressive stages:

### Stage I — 'Poor' Air Quality (Delhi AQI 201 - 300)
- Ensure strict enforcement of dust control measures at construction & demolition (C&D) sites.
- Mechanized sweeping and water sprinkling on unpaved roads and identified hotspots.
- Divert non-destined vehicular traffic away from congested central zones.
- Enforce prohibition on open burning of municipal solid waste, biomass, and dry leaves.
- Ensure strict compliance with PUC (Pollution Under Control) norms.

### Stage II — 'Very Poor' Air Quality (Delhi AQI 301 - 400)
- Intensify mechanized road sweeping and water sprinkling with dust suppressants at identified pollution hotspots.
- Ensure uninterrupted power supply to eliminate the operation of commercial Diesel Generator (DG) sets.
- Traffic management: Synchronize traffic signals and deploy traffic marshals at congested bottlenecks to avoid vehicular idling.
- Regulate entry of non-essential commercial goods vehicles into Delhi and divert transit trucks via the Eastern and Western Peripheral Expressways (EPE and WPE).
- Public advisory: Urge citizens to use public transit, carpool, and minimize personal motor vehicle trips.

### Stage III — 'Severe' Air Quality (Delhi AQI 401 - 450)
- Strict ban on all C&D activities across Delhi-NCR (excluding essential public infrastructure projects).
- Ban on the plying of BS-III Petrol and BS-IV Diesel Light Motor Vehicles (LMVs) / 4-wheelers in Delhi and adjoining districts.
- Ban on entry of Light Commercial Vehicles (LCVs) registered outside Delhi, other than EVs, CNG, and BS-VI diesel vehicles.
- Mining and stone crushing operations completely closed.
- Intensified frequency of metro and municipal bus services to accommodate diverted commuter volume.

### Stage IV — 'Severe+' Air Quality (Delhi AQI > 450)
- Prohibit entry of all truck traffic into Delhi except those carrying essential commodities or providing essential services. All LNG/CNG/electric trucks permitted.
- Stop entry of non-Delhi registered LCVs other than EVs/CNG/BS-VI diesel.
- Ban on plying of Delhi-registered diesel Medium Goods Vehicles (MGVs) and Heavy Goods Vehicles (HGVs), except those carrying essential goods.
- Suspension of physical classes for primary schools (Classes up to V or up to IX/XI as decided by GNCTD).
- State governments may consider work-from-home (WFH) directives for 50% of staff in public and private offices.
