# Health Advisory Protocols for Sensitive & Vulnerable Populations

Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/health-advisory-guidelines/
Retrieval Date: 2026-11-15

## Vulnerable Population Segments
1. **Children & Infants:** Higher respiratory rate per kilogram of body weight; developing lung alveoli and immune systems make them acutely vulnerable to PM2.5 and NO2 exposure.
2. **Elderly (65+ years):** Pre-existing cardio-respiratory conditions, reduced vascular elasticity, and diminished mucociliary clearance.
3. **Individuals with Chronic Conditions:** Patients suffering from bronchial asthma, COPD, coronary artery disease, and diabetes.
4. **Pregnant Women:** Increased risk of preterm birth, low birth weight, and maternal hypertensive disorders.

## Actionable Protective Directives
- **School Zones Protection:** In Poor, Very Poor, and Severe AQI conditions, heavy vehicular transit within a 500-meter perimeter of schools should be diverted. Outdoor physical training and sports must be suspended when AQI exceeds 300.
- **Commuter Precautions:** When driving through high pollution corridors, roll up vehicle windows and switch ventilation to internal recirculation mode with high-efficiency cabin filters.
- **Exposure Reduction Measures:** Route planning that shifts commuters from high-congestion corridors (e.g. Anand Vihar, Vikas Marg) to cleaner suburban peripheries can reduce peak particulate inhalation by 15% to 30%, significantly mitigating acute respiratory irritation.
